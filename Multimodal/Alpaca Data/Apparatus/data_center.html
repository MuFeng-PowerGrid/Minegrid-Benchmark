Data Center Load

AFE, UPS DC link, VSI, PSU, equivalent computing load, and backup power dynamics.

Model scope

The boundary covers the power-electronic supply chain and equivalent computing load: active front end (AFE), UPS DC link, voltage-source inverter (VSI), power supply unit (PSU), and equivalent load. The engineering configuration adds measurement and actuator dynamics, current/conductance commands, backup-battery supply, and stored energy. Server scheduling and detailed thermal-fluid behavior are outside this boundary.

Detailed entry: Detailed engineering configuration. Alternative control configurations retain their own state and equation sets.
