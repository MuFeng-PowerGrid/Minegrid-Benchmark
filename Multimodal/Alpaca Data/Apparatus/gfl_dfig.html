Grid-Following DFIG Wind Turbine

Doubly fed wind generation with grid-following rotor-side and grid-side controls.

Model scope

Rotor-side and grid-side controls address machine speed/reactive current and the DC link with grid-following synchronization. Aerodynamic, drivetrain, magnetic-flux, and converter-actuator dynamics are retained. The detailed configuration includes pitch, crowbar, current limiting, fault ride-through, and power recovery; its branch selection uses ird_limit=False.
