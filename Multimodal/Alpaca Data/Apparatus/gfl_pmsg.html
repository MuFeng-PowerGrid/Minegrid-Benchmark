Grid-Following Direct-Drive PMSG Wind Turbine

Permanent-magnet wind generation with a PLL-based grid-side converter.

Model scope

Machine-side speed/current control is coupled to grid-side PLL, DC-voltage, and current regulation. The model retains permanent-magnet machine fluxes, rotor speed, DC-link voltage, filter dynamics, and converter actuation. The detailed configuration includes pitch, fault ride-through, and active-power recovery.
