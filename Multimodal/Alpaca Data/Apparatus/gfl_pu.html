Grid-Following Converter: PU

PLL synchronization with active-power and AC-voltage control.

Model scope

The PLL establishes synchronization, while the outer loops regulate active power and AC-voltage magnitude. Electrical, actuator, and fault-support dynamics are retained. The second reference is Ut_ref, an AC-voltage target. Sequence-control variants have their own state and equation sets.

Detailed entry: Detailed engineering configuration. Alternative control configurations retain their own state and equation sets.
