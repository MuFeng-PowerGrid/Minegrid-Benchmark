Grid-Forming Converter: Droop

Power-filter and droop synchronization with inner control loops, electrical storage, and actuator dynamics.

Model scope

Filtered active power and the P–f droop law determine synchronization. Reactive-power/voltage control is coupled to the inner loops, LCL filter, and DC link. This branch has its own droop law; the separate primary-frequency overlay is specific to the VSG configuration.
