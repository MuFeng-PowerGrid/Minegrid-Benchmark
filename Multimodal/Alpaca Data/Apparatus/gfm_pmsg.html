Grid-Forming Direct-Drive PMSG Wind Turbine

Permanent-magnet wind generation with machine-side control and grid-forming converter variants.

Model scope

The machine-side and grid-side converters are coupled by a DC-link capacitor. Mechanical wind input and electromagnetic torque close the energy balance. The primary detailed configuration uses VSG control. A separate detailed configuration uses ReferenceControl=1 and squared-DC-voltage matching, including pitch, fault ride-through, and its native negative-sequence control.

Detailed entry: Detailed engineering configuration. Alternative control configurations retain their own state and equation sets.
