AC/DC Interlinking Converter

AC/DC energy exchange with PQ, EQ, PU, and EU outer-control configurations.

Model scope

The four outer-control modes regulate P/Q, DC voltage/Q, P/AC voltage, and DC voltage/AC voltage, respectively. PLL, filter, and DC-side energy relationships are retained. These outer-loop modes are mutually exclusive. Each mode also has a sequence-control extension; a separate zero-sequence state or port exists only where explicitly listed.

Detailed entry: EU control with active sequence control. Alternative control configurations retain their own state and equation sets.
