Detailed Synchronous Generator

Six-winding electromagnetic dynamics, rotor motion, governor, excitation system, and power system stabilizer.

Model scope

The six winding fluxes determine stator, field, and damper currents and couple electromagnetic torque to rotor motion. The 25-state configuration includes an IEEEG1-style governor with valve-rate and valve-position limits, an ESST3A-style exciter with a piecewise rectifier characteristic, and PSS input-filter and lead-lag states. The alternative 16-state configuration includes separate saturation and engineering-limit features. In the 25-state formulation, EnableSaturation, THETAP, and the low-pressure turbine term PLP do not provide fully independent additional dynamics; their names alone do not imply those features are active.

Detailed entry: Six-winding generator with IEEEG1-style governor, ESST3A-style exciter, and PSS. Alternative control configurations retain their own state and equation sets.
